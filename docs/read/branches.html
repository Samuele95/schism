Two branches

Two branches

  • Weights branch — open-weight models loaded locally or on a remote GPU. The edit is a change to the matrices that write into the residual stream, stored as a LoRA adapter on a scratch copy and never written over the original. Preservation is measured as first-token KL on neutral prompts.
  • Semantic branch — closed models with no weights to touch. The edit is a prompt operator; every geometric quantity is measured on embeddings of sampled responses; preservation is drift as a multiple of the sampling noise floor. Results are worded "with this operator", never as a change to the model.

The full recipe for closed models is in the semantic-branch card; the residual stream and its writers are in the stream card.
