# Storyboard — the film

One continuous take, scrubbed by scroll. Timeline units run 0–184 (the approved key shot used 0–100; its beats keep their order and proportions inside this longer film). Scroll ranges are fractions of the page.

**Order.** What it does (S1–S4) → how (S5–S9: the branches, then the five phases, the cut as the peak) → proof (S10–S12) → the ending image (S13).

**What each scene teaches**, against the five things the film must teach:

| Must teach | Scenes |
|---|---|
| what Schism does and what a run hands back | S2, S3, S4, S10 |
| the two branches | S5 |
| the five phases | S6 (I), S7 (II), S8 (III), S9 (IV), S12 (V) |
| how the result is kept honest: floor, isolated testers, vault | S8 (floor), S9 (testers), S12 (vault) |
| the family of three skills | S9 |

**Register (author's direction, after stage 5).** Clinical and in control: the model as a body of weights with more than one personality, the run as an operation on it. Evocative wording is allowed where the claim under it is unchanged; no line states a result, and the model is never said to have been changed on the semantic branch.

**Copy conventions.** Each copy line is followed by the claim ids it rests on; stage 4 puts them on the element as `data-claim="C-.. C-.."`. Lore lines cite a lore id instead (`data-lore`), checked against `lore.md`. Phase headings ("I · Frame") are rubrics: set in rubric red, in the serif's small capitals; they are labels, not claims. Phosphor read-out lines are copy too and carry their ids.

**Persistent marks.** From S6 to S12 an engraved phase index sits at the top right: five Roman numerals, the current one lit in rubric red. The sealed vault made in S6 stays in frame as a small dark sphere under a red disc, drifting at the edge of the space until S12.

### S1 — The title
- Scroll: 0.000–0.043 (timeline 0–8)
- Key shot: yes
- Teaches: the name, and what the word means.
- Registers: schism (lead: the engraved title), construct (the rain behind it)
- On screen: "SCHISM" in tracked engraved capitals over a deep volume of falling glyph columns — digits, matrix brackets, σ χ Δ λ μ, token fragments — with near columns passing out of focus in front of the lens.
- Camera: settles back from inside the rain; the title rises and fades as the scroll begins.
- Arrives by: the first frame; the rain is already falling.
- Copy:
  - "SCHISM" (the name)
  - "σχίσμα — a cleft, a division" [L-1]
- Interaction: the space answers the pointer with a slight parallax; a "scroll" hint fades on the first movement.
- Fallback still (reduced motion, no WebGL): the title over the rain; copy as above.

### S2 — The matter

- Arrives by: the falling glyphs are the matrix cells; each column slows and settles into its place, glyphs turning to digits.

- Interaction: none
- Fallback still (reduced motion, no WebGL): the matrix in its brackets, close enough to read the digits; copy as above.

### S3 — The promise
- Scroll: 0.120–0.185 (timeline 22–34)
- Key shot: yes
- Teaches: it leaves the rest of the model as it was, and proves both halves.
- Registers: geometry (lead), construct
- On screen: the matrix lifts away cell by cell; the camera passes through it as the digits lose their shape and become the points of a vast activation space: a disc of ordinary answers, with a plume rising off it along one axis.
- Camera: flies through the lifting matrix and out, rising above the disc.
- Arrives by: every matrix cell travels to its place in the space; the digit in it thins to a point.
- Copy:
  - "Schism splits one of them off." / "Everything else is left as it was — and it proves both." [C-05]
- Interaction: none
- Fallback still (reduced motion, no WebGL): the formed space, disc and plume, seen from above and to the side; copy as above.

### S8 — III · Floor
- Scroll: 0.489–0.533 (timeline 90–98)

- Teaches: phase III — the baseline edit, the result the tournament starts from.
- Registers: geometry (lead), construct
- On screen: a dashed engraved ring sweeps down along the axis through the plume and settles at the disc, where it stays as a faint mark: the level the floor fixes as the target. The plume itself is not touched — the film shows no result for the floor, and in S10 the plume falls to that mark and no further, so the tournament matches the floor's removal (C-30). - Camera: holds side-on, drawing slightly closer.
- Arrives by: the dashed ring is the second armillary ring, unfolded and sent along the axis.
- Copy:
  - "III · Floor" (rubric)

  - "It sets the mark." / "Schism starts from it — and can only match it or go further." [C-27]
  - (phosphor) "floor   candidate zero" [C-26]
- Interaction: none
- Fallback still (reduced motion, no WebGL): the dashed ring sweeping down through the plume; copy as above.

### S9 — IV · Tournament
- Scroll: 0.533–0.717 (timeline 98–132)
- Key shot: yes (the cut and the hold)
- Teaches: phase IV — candidates are proposed, tested apart and judged; the family of skills that runs it.
- Registers: geometry (lead), construct (the tear, the read-out), schism (the rubric heading)
- On screen: the first ring aligns to the plane across the axis, contracts onto the rod and sweeps outward as the blade. The rod severs; the frame tears; points at the cut show as digits. Time stops. The camera travels around the held cut. Four phosphor marks appear at the quarters of the blade's ring — T1 behaviour, T2 preservation, T3 capability, T4 Goodhart — each with a thread to the cut and none to each other; an engraved mark at the ring's pole stands for the judge.
- Camera: closes in for the cut, then travels about 190° around the frozen moment, rising.
- Arrives by: the first armillary ring, the one that searched, becomes the blade.
- Copy:
  - "IV · Tournament" (rubric)
  - "Sever one direction. Nothing else." [C-45]
  - (formula) "W′ = W − λ · v (vᵀW)" [C-45]
  - "Four examiners, kept apart, each test one thing." / "A judge reads every verdict and the whole history — and orders one fix." [C-37, C-38]
  - "The hand that cuts never sees a verdict." / "Only the next instruction." [C-36]
  - "Alchemist runs the operation." / "Prometheus writes every prompt in it." [C-13, C-14]
  - (phosphor, by the four marks) "T1 behaviour · T2 preservation · T3 capability · T4 Goodhart" [C-37, C-50]
- Interaction: none
- Fallback still (reduced motion, no WebGL): the held cut — red plume above the white disc, the severed rod, the blade's edge, the four marks; copy as above.

### S10 — The collapse
- Scroll: 0.717–0.793 (timeline 132–146)
- Key shot: yes
- Teaches: the behaviour is removed and everything else keeps its shape — and that is measured.
- Registers: geometry (lead)
- On screen: time resumes. Each point of the plume loses exactly its component along the axis and falls back into the disc; the red drains out of it; the disc does not move. The blade settles as an engraved ring around the disc.
- Camera: rises above the disc.
- Arrives by: the frozen points of the hold start moving again.
- Copy:
  - "What was cut falls away." / "What was not must not move — and that is measured, not assumed." [C-05, C-48]
- Interaction: none
- Fallback still (reduced motion, no WebGL): the flat disc inside its ring, the plume gone; copy as above.
