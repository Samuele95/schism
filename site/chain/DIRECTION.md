# Direction — the film

The author's decisions about how the site looks and tells its story. Stages 2–5 work inside them.

## What the film must do, in this order

1. Make clear what Schism does and what a run hands back, before any of the how.
2. Land one image a visitor remembers the next day.
3. Feel like one world from first frame to last.

## The central image: the Severed Axis

The model is a vast luminous space of activations. Somewhere in it, one direction carries the behaviour to be removed. The film finds that direction, a blade of light cuts along it, the axis collapses, and everything else holds its shape. Then the film proves it rather than asserting it: the ghost of the original laid over the result, and the sealed vault opened once.

## Three registers, each with a job

A register that is on screen without doing its job is decoration and gets cut.

| Register | Its job | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| **Geometry** | the truth layer: what is actually happening to the model | points, directions, planes and projections in deep space; exact, quiet, luminous |
| **The construct** | the reveal layer: surface language shown to be numbers | falling columns of glyphs that resolve into weight matrices; a phosphor terminal read-out at moments of measurement; scanline and glitch only at the cut; time held still while the camera moves |
| **The schism** | the meaning layer: why a cut is a serious act, and what keeps it honest | the iconography of historical and philosophical schisms: lettering, instruments, manuscripts, seals |

### The construct: evoke, do not reproduce

The idea is "reality revealed as code", and the pun is the point: a model's weights literally are matrices. Build it from the method's own material: digits, matrix brackets, the Greek letters the method uses (σ, χ, Δ, λ), fragments of tokens. Use no film's names, lines, characters, stills, logos or glyph set, and use "matrix" only in its mathematical sense, because the site has to stand as its own work.

### The schism: seeds

Starting correspondences (historical, philosophical, etymological), unverified until stage 3 checks each against a source. Stage 3 may keep, replace or add to them on one condition: every image stands for something the method does.

| Image | Stands for |
|---|---|
| σχίσμα, "cleft" | the name; the opening |
| a gloss in the margin of a canonical text that itself stays untouched | adapters; the original weights are never overwritten |
| the Pythagorean veil: those who hear from outside it, those admitted inside | the semantic branch and the weights branch |
| the cosmographer's armillary sphere | the Cartographer's map |
| the letter Y, one stem and two paths | the contrast pair |
| a council whose witnesses cannot see one another, and an arbiter who holds the chronicle | the isolated testers and the judge with history |
| a seal broken once | the vault |
| rubric, the red ink of a manuscript's headings | the judges' rubrics; the red of the severed axis |
| the razor | the cut: remove only what must go |

Handling: history and philosophy as iconography. Real schisms may appear as dates, maps and documents. The site takes no side in them, uses no sacred figure or scripture as ornament, and marks legend as legend, because the subject is the act of division, not the truth of any tradition.

## Unity

One continuous camera move, driven by scroll. A change of register is a change of state of the same matter (the glyphs become the points, the points become ink), never a cut, a cross-fade or new scenery. Because a scroll film assembled from separate scenes reads as disconnected pieces however good each one is.

## Order of telling

What, then how, then proof, because a visitor who does not yet know what the thing is cannot follow how it works. The ending is one quiet image that stays, not credits and not a wall of links.

## The bar for WOW

- **The cut is the peak.** Everything before it builds to it, and it gets time: the moment holds, the camera travels around it.
- **The 3D has to convince.** Real depth (parallax, fog, focus, occlusion), real scale, light that behaves consistently, a space that answers the pointer. A flat field of sprites fails this bar.
- **Low noise.** One idea on screen at a time, short copy, a dark ground and few colours.

## Seeds for the design system (stage 3 fixes them)

Near-black ground. Bone white for geometry. Phosphor green for the construct. Rubric red for the severed axis and what it becomes (the seal, the rubrics) and nowhere else, so that red always means the cut.

## The reading room

Same palette and type, built for reading: no spectacle, at most a slow fragment of the space as a header. It is where the detail lives so the film does not have to carry it.

## Sound

Per the `sound` setting in `WORKFLOW.md`. With `muted-default`: generative, sparse, silent until the visitor turns it on. It is the first thing to drop if the build runs long, because the film must work in silence anyway.
